Terms & Definitions

1. What's the term for burning gas as a by-product during refining?   > Flaring
2. What temperature is gas cooled to for LNG, and by how much does that shrink its volume?   > -162C, shrinks volume 600 times
3. What's the cooler in liquefaction called?   > Refrigerant (the cooler used in liquefaction)
4. What's the process of turning LNG back to gas called?   > Regasification
5. What process uses a liquid catalyst to crack big molecules into gasoline/diesel?   > Fluid Catalytic Cracking (FCC) - liquid catalyst cracks big molecules into gasoline/diesel

History & Figures
6. Who started the first refinery, and in what year?   > Abraham Gesner started the first refinery, 1846
7. Who discovered oil in Titusville, Pennsylvania, and what well is named after him?   > Colonel Edwin L. Drake discovered oil in Titusville, Pennsylvania, 1859 - the Drake Well
8. Who coined "Seven Sisters," and what's the other name for that group?   > Enrico Mattei coined Seven Sisters, also called Anglo-Saxon
9. What year did the oil crisis occur?   > 1973 - the oil crisis

Abbreviations
10. What does FPSO stand for?   > FPSO = Floating, Production, Storage and Offloading
11. What does GSA stand for, and where does it fit in the FDP process?   > GSA = Gas Sale Agreement, secured alongside FDP before FID
Structure & Companies

12. What are the 3 sub-parts of the upstream sector?   > Upstream 3 sub-parts: Exploration, Development, Production
13. Give one example of an NOC and one IOC.   > NOC example: Saudi Aramco. IOC example: ExxonMobil or Shell
14. What's the key difference between an Operator (E&P) company and a Service company?   > 
Operator/E&P owns rights to oil/gas extracted. Service companies never own the hydrocarbons, paid for equipment and expertise only

Reserve Confidence
15. If Proved (P90) is 40 million barrels, is the P10 number bigger or smaller — and why?   > P10 is bigger - optimistic high end of range, only 10 percent chance of exceeding it
16. What percentage of oil (vs. gas) is typically recoverable from a reservoir?   > About 30 percent oil, 70 percent gas typically recoverable
